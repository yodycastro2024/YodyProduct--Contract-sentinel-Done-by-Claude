# Trust-Gap Mitigations · Contract Sentinel

## Trust gaps
| Gap | Where it shows up | User cost | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hallucination | An invented "position" or a paraphrased citation | A waived right if it reaches sales | Verification gate: verbatim playbook citation or "Unverified"; unverified can't be logged |
| Opacity (no "why") | A severity with no reasoning | Lawyer re-reads the whole clause, trust erodes | Every flag shows its cited clause + rationale, expandable to the playbook text |
| No user control | The tool decides and the lawyer can't intervene | Approval-fatigue click-through, or rejection of the tool | Per-flag override, confirm gate before logging, send hard-blocked |

## Highest-priority fix
**Hallucination**, closed by the verification gate. In legal, an invented position that looks authoritative is the failure that ends the product; grounding-or-abstaining is the one non-negotiable.
