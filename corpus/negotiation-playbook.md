# Meridian Contract Negotiation Playbook (excerpt)

## Liability cap
Preferred: liability capped at 12 months' fees. Acceptable: cap at total contract value. Walk-away: uncapped or unlimited liability, or any carve-out that makes liability effectively unlimited.

## Indemnification
Preferred: mutual indemnification limited to third-party IP and confidentiality breaches. Walk-away: a one-sided indemnity that runs only in the counterparty's favour with no reciprocal obligation.

## Payment terms
Preferred: Net-30 from invoice. Acceptable: Net-45. Walk-away: Net-60 or longer, or payment tied to the end of a quarter rather than the invoice date.

## Auto-renewal
Preferred: renewal with a 60-day opt-out notice window. Acceptable: 30-day notice. Walk-away: automatic renewal with no opt-out, or a notice window shorter than 15 days.

## Data and privacy
Required: counterparty signs our DPA; data processed only in approved regions; breach notification within 72 hours. Walk-away: no breach-notification commitment.

## IP ownership
Preferred: each party keeps its background IP; the customer owns its data, Meridian owns the platform. Walk-away: any assignment of Meridian platform IP or a broad work-for-hire clause.

## Governing law
Preferred: Delaware. Acceptable: New York. Walk-away: a foreign jurisdiction where neither party has a business nexus.
